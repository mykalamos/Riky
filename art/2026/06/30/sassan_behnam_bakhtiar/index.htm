Sassan Behnam-Bakhtiar
SA 080526
Mixed media on linen
120 x 100 cm

Some things exist precisely because others no longer do. Sedimentary rocks, for example, are derivative of more ancient matter transformed by disintegration and pressurised recombination. In this work, Behnam-Bakhtiar's own lithification technique persuades the rainbow to stiffen into vertical columns.

The artist does not permit a smooth lane structure with a horizontal cleft dividing the canvas. The spines of unrelated books in a dusty library powdered by a prism.

Peering into this opencast mine, the orange-red bands vie with the peeling blue and the yellow-green zest. Decay is already initiated. Elsewhere off-whites and their familiars coexist more amicably.

The fine brushwork and extensive palette use finer striations to build coarser ones. There is a fricative mood as crystalline

Seen at Setareh London on 11 June 2026. Part of a solo exhibition 'Sanctuary' on show until 11 July.
